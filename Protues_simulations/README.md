# Proteus Circuit Simulations

## Objective

To design and simulate analog circuits using Proteus and analyze their functional behavior.


## Circuits Implemented

###  Instrumentation Amplifier

* Designed a differential amplifier for signal conditioning
* Provides high input impedance and accurate amplification of small signals
* also provides high CMRR


### 🔹 Band-Pass Filter

* Designed a frequency-selective circuit
* Allows signals within a specific frequency range to pass while attenuating others

## 📊 Simulation Details

* Tool used: Proteus
* Analysis performed:

  * Input-output behavior
  * Frequency response (for filter circuit)


##  Results and Observations

* Instrumentation amplifier demonstrated stable gain and effective differential signal amplification
* Band-pass filter showed clear passband and attenuation regions


##  Key Learnings

* Importance of differential amplification in real-world systems
* Frequency-selective behavior of filters
* Practical circuit visualization and debugging
